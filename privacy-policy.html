Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when booking travel services, including name, contact details, passport information, and payment details. This information is essential for processing your travel bookings.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process bookings, communicate about your travel plans, provide customer support, and improve our services. We may also send you travel updates and promotional offers with your consent.

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only with trusted partners necessary for your travel arrangements, including airlines, hotels, and visa processing agencies. Anaya Escapes never sells your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All payment transactions are processed through secure, encrypted channels to ensure your financial safety.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. Travel records are typically maintained for 7 years as required by travel industry regulations.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us directly.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, contact us at Sales@anayaescapes.com or call +91 84484 24042.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your travel data is handled with the utmost care and security.
