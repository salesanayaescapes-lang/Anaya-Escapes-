About Us

Dubai

At Anaya Escapes, we believe that travel is not just about reaching a destination — it's about the experiences, memories, and stories you gather along the way. With a passion for exploration and over 5 years of expertise, we have crafted journeys for 300+ happy travelers across 15+ breathtaking destinations. From serene beaches to vibrant cities, we curate each trip with precision, ensuring every moment is as unforgettable as the place itself.

Our team is dedicated to providing end-to-end travel solutions — from planning your itinerary and booking flights to arranging accommodations and securing visas. We pride ourselves on offering personalized services that match your interests, budget, and travel style. Whether it's a romantic honeymoon, a fun family getaway, or a corporate trip, we go the extra mile to make sure your journey is smooth, stress-free, and filled with remarkable memories.
